## 生成来歴

built-in image_genで確定画像を参照し4表情を個別編集。平静は添付PNGをそのままコピー。画像の手加工は行わない。CC0取得済みやレイヤー原画の存在を主張しない。出力間に生成由来の微小な輪郭差がある。

### 共通プロンプト

Edit this APPROVED mascot reference. Output ONE transparent full-body character, identical canvas/framing, scale, position, 1.5-head-tall round proportions, navy hat/robe, gold trim, white hair/moustache/beard, fan and hands and feet and pose. Preserve everything except facial expression and specified sweat/tears. NO NOSE at all. Keep signature eyes as upright white outer oval enclosing dark inner oval, adjust pupils/eyebrows to show emotion while retaining double-oval style. No text or extra characters. Actual alpha transparent background, no ground shadow. 

### smug

Expression: boastfully smug, proud triumphant grin with open happy mouth, eyebrows lifted confidently, pupils look slightly sideways, rosy cheeks. Lovable cocky grandpa.

### rattled

Expression: worried and rattled, eyebrows lifted toward center, wide double-oval eyes with smaller pupils, small trembling open mouth and ONE blue sweat drop at temple. Clearly concerned but cute.

### meltdown

Expression: complete comic panic and tearful defeat, tiny oval pupils within wide oval eyes, uneven worried eyebrows, mouth opens wide wailing, one blue tear stream on each cheek. Extremely flustered, still cute.

### coverUp

Expression: pretending everything is fine despite embarrassment, both oval pupils look to the side, one raised eyebrow, strained closed crooked smile under moustache, ONE small blue sweat drop. Awkward forced confidence, distinct from frightened rattled.
